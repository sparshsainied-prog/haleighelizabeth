Bring Sadie to your class

Haleigh was a classroom teacher before she wrote Sadie. Her visits mix read-alouds, hands-on STEM activities and a lot of questions, for schools, libraries and community groups.

  1. Classroom readings. About 20 minutes in each class on the day.
  2. Grade level assembly. A reading and a short talk for a big group, about 45 minutes.
  3. Writing workshop. Small groups take one idea and build it into a story map, about 30 minutes each.
  4. Literacy night. An evening for families on helping with reading at home. It pairs with any of the others.
